# TeenTaal-FM1

An Indian classical music practice firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1: a tanpura, tabla playing ten taals, and a reference
tone. By **ElectroNick** — [github.com/ELENICK97](https://github.com/ELENICK97).

**AI-generated and tested only in emulators, never on a real FM-1. Use it at your own risk.** A failed update can
leave an FM-1 unable to start. Read the [disclaimer](https://github.com/ELENICK97/TeenTaal-FM1#disclaimer) and the
[installation guide](https://github.com/ELENICK97/TeenTaal-FM1/blob/main/INSTALL.md) first, and keep the official V15
firmware at hand.

(Freesound, CC0). Tanpura after van Walstijn, Bridges and Mehes, DAFx-16. Not affiliated with M-VAVE.
